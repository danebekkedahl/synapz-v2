# SYNAPZ HOCKEY V2.0 — STUDIO NORTH STAR

## Mission
Build the world's cleanest, fastest-to-grasp **tablet-first off-ice Hockey IQ training game and coaching studio** (`1024×768` / `1180×820` iPad landscape primary, responsive across portrait tablets, phones, and desktop).

Every screen must pass the **2-Second Arena Test**:
1. **Zero text-wall quizzes** — Players read body hips, stick blades, lanes, and ice space visually, never paragraphs or multiple-choice A/B/C buttons.
2. **Athletic touch gestures** — Tap open tape for a snap pass, curve-swipe to saucer over sticks or bank off boards, drag your puck-carrier to walk a seam, or flick to shoot.
3. **Instant physics consequence + Telestrator Rewind** — Every decision plays out at 100% live speed so players *watch* why a play worked or got picked off, backed by a 1.5s glowing Telestrator cue (`< 12 words`).
4. **Universal developmental scale (`8U/U7 Novice` -> `18U/Jr/Pro`, `0–1000+ IQ`)** — Adaptive speed, stick/hip deception, bullet-time duration, and assist rings automatically meet every player at their exact edge.

---

## Core Design Laws
- **Zero-Friction Boot:** Cold launch straight into the dark-arena **Tablet Command Hub** — 1 tap to drop the puck, 0 login walls.
- **Tablet Ergonomics:** Locked `100dvh` arena viewport (`0px` unintended scroll), `>= 52px` touch targets across all rails, pills, and skater handles.
- **Sound-Optional Clarity:** Procedural Web Audio arena foley (skate carve, snap pass, saucer whoosh, shin block, post ping, goal horn, bullet-time heartbeat) paired 1:1 with a non-blocking **Telestrator Caption Bar** for muted tablets.
- **Redemption Learning Loop:** Mistakes automatically schedule a mirrored/rotated **Redemption Rep** 3 plays later to build true spatial transfer instead of memorization.

---

## The 4 Game Modes & 16-Pillar Mastery Radar
1. **`⚡ LIVE READ`** — Live roll-in -> 25% bullet-time touch read -> 100% live physics outcome + automatic Telestrator Rewind.
2. **`🧲 POSITION & SPACING LAB`** — Interactive live puck-drag Tactical Table + *"Where Do I Belong?"* off-puck spatial game.
3. **`👀 HEAD-UP SCANNER`** — Pre-reception shoulder-check window -> peripheral fog on reception -> instant one-touch outlet flick.
4. **`📋 COACH & 3v3 STUDIO`** — `< 30s` iPad draw + 10s voice/caption pairing -> 1-tap `▶ Play This Rep Live` + JSON Team Play Packs.
5. **`📊 MY HOCKEY IQ`** — 16-Pillar visual mastery radar, live `0–1000+` IQ score, streak, and Saturday Ice Transfer habits.
